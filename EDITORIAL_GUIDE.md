# ADNOC Partnership News: what we include

Claude reads this guide to decide which stories to **keep** or **drop** before
writing any bullets.

**To improve it:** paste headlines from past ADNOC bulletins into the "Past headlines"
lists. The more real examples there are, the better the keep/drop judgement gets.
The examples below are real stories, but they come from news coverage, not from
past bulletins.

The bulletin goes to a senior ADNOC client audience in the UAE. Every story should
be one an ADNOC sponsorship or communications professional would want to know about
today: news about ADNOC's own properties, what competing brands are doing in sport,
and the big moves in the sports it's involved in.

**Audience note:** the readers follow the UAE closely. Stories with an Emirati angle
(Emirati athletes such as Rashid Al Dhaheri or Emirati riders at UAE Team Emirates
XRG, UAE clubs, the UAE national team, events hosted in Abu Dhabi) get a higher
priority than an equally sized story with no UAE link.

---

## Never included (any section)

- Local, grassroots or club-level news with no ADNOC link: a school sports day,
  a community club's fundraiser or new clubhouse. (ADNOC's own community
  programmes, such as the UAE Team Emirates XRG Academy, are included.)
- Equipment, product or gear reviews, buying guides and "best of" lists.
- Betting tips, odds, predictions, fantasy picks, quizzes and puzzles.
- Racecards and horse-racing tips.
- Opinion columns, rankings of players or "talking points" pieces with no new facts.
- Match previews or team-news pieces that only repeat known information.
- Live blogs, video or highlights pages, galleries and podcasts.
- Celebrity, lifestyle or gossip pieces.
- The same story already covered by another outlet. Keep the best version,
  preferring the section's preferred sources and, for UAE stories, UAE outlets
  (The National, WAM, Gulf News, Khaleej Times).

## ADNOC & Partner News

**Keep:** ADNOC's (and XRG's) own sports deals and activations: new deals, renewals,
extensions, exits and campaign launches ("Energy in Every Move"). Its properties
include the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix (founding partner), the ADNOC Pro League (title
partner), the ADNOC Abu Dhabi Marathon (title partner), UAE Team Emirates XRG and
its academy, the Abu Dhabi Gold Cup, Challenge Sir Bani Yas and the Open Masters
Games. Also keep news about ADNOC-backed athletes such as Rashid Al Dhaheri.

**Drop:** ADNOC's oil, gas, finance, trading, acquisitions and corporate news, and
general energy-industry news. If a story only mentions ADNOC in passing, drop it.

- Zawya: ADNOC announced as headline partner for Challenge Sir Bani Yas 2026
- UAE Team Emirates: UAE Team Emirates-XRG and ADNOC launch community cycling program

## Competitor News

**Keep:** one of the tracked brands (see SUMMARY_STYLE.md) doing a sponsorship,
partnership, naming-rights or activation deal in **sport**, anywhere in the world.
That includes new deals, renewals, extensions, exits, athlete programmes and
criticism of those deals.

**Drop:** earnings, share prices, takeovers, oil and gas operations, general business
news, ADNOC's own deals, and any company that isn't on the brand list. Results from
events that carry a brand's name (DP World Tour golf, the Mubadala Citi DC Open) are
dropped unless the story is about the sponsorship itself.

## Wider Sports News

**Keep:** sports-industry and trade news, which doesn't need to involve ADNOC: rights
deals, sponsorship and naming-rights announcements, media partnerships, event hosting
and bids, governing-body decisions, league and club commercial moves, and agency or
brand moves. Gulf sports-business stories (Saudi, Qatari and UAE hosting, investment
and sponsorship moves) get priority.

**Drop:** match reports, transfer gossip and player news (unless it's a major
commercial story), and general business or politics.

## Formula 1 & Motorsport

**Keep:** the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix and Yas Marina Circuit; F1 race results,
championship standings and major driver or team news; F1 commercial and sponsorship
news; Rashid Al Dhaheri and other Emirati drivers; and the feeder series he races in
or comes through (F2, F3, F4 UAE, Formula Regional Middle East, F1 Academy).

**Drop:** minor technical or set-up pieces, fan-merchandise stories, and other
motorsport series (NASCAR, IndyCar, MotoGP) unless it's a major commercial story or
has a UAE angle.

Note: ADNOC doesn't sponsor an F1 team. Don't treat team-sponsor stories as ADNOC news.
